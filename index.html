04 / CONTROLLED TRANSITIONS

Move context.
Not unchecked power.

A task can cross environments without carrying its secrets or expanding its permissions. Destination-bound checkpoints are signed, replay-protected, and constrained.

  • Transfer task references and artifact hashes.
  • Require fresh authority at the destination.
  • Leave private keys, funds, and credentials behind.

This is a checkpoint handoff—not live process migration. No running agent or execution authority is transferred.
